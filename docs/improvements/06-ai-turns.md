# 06 — Opponent / AI Turns

Goal: make AI intelligence visible and entertaining without manufacturing long waits.

- **AI-01** Give every AI turn a clear start: player hand recedes, opponent seat/avatar activates, name/turn cue appears briefly.
- **AI-02** Use personality-specific pre-move behavior instead of generic `thinking...`.
- **AI-03** Keep presentation pause short; expose character mainly through timing/reaction, not fake long computation.
- **AI-04** Animate hidden cards from opponent seat as card backs and reveal at destination.
- **AI-05** Animate meaningful existing-table rearrangements, not only final state replacement.
- **AI-06** Let animation duration scale with information content: draw fast, simple play medium, complex Mexe longer.
- **AI-07** Spend more time showing the move than pretending the AI is thinking.
- **AI-08** Strengthen post-move changed-card highlighting and retain a short reading beat before player control returns.
- **AI-09** Present AI explanation as concise tactical/character commentary; detailed learning mode may explain strategy.
- **AI-10** Keep reactions beside avatars and after the move so they never obscure card information.
- **AI-11** Make near-win 2/1-card states visibly tense.
- **AI-12** Give forced-draw reactions character-specific presentation while keeping the draw itself fast.
- **AI-13** Allow experienced players/settings to accelerate long presentations.
- **AI-14** For huge rearrangements, animate representative/grouped phases rather than every mutation serially.
- **AI-15** Give genuine multi-meld rearrangements a rare signature `MEXEU!` moment.
- **AI-16** Let opponents occasionally react to unusually strong player Mexe turns, with cooldowns.
- **AI-17** Later/local persistence: support lightweight head-to-head rivalry records if product scope includes it.

Verification: draw-only AI, simple meld, complex rearrangement, all personalities, near-win, fast AI setting, reduced motion.

# 09 — Win / Results / Rematch

Goal: celebrate the result, explain the match story, and make another game effortless.

- **RESULT-01** Stage celebration first, then stats/controls; avoid competing information in the first ~1 s.
- **RESULT-02** Elevate the winning move into the headline story, not a minor line.
- **RESULT-03** Show at most one meaningful match-story label such as `VIRADA`, `NO APERTO`, `MEXEU BONITO`, `MESA VIRADA`.
- **RESULT-04** Make `JOGAR DE NOVO` / `REVANCHE` the dominant CTA; `TROCAR JOGADORES` secondary; Menu tertiary.
- **RESULT-05** Preserve opponent/social continuity and use short character-specific rematch reactions where supported.
- **RESULT-06** If persistence scope allows, show lightweight head-to-head rivalry history.
- **RESULT-07** Show only stats that describe how the match felt; avoid dumping every telemetry field.
- **RESULT-08** Compare player/opponent stats when comparison creates meaning.
- **RESULT-09** Keep the final table visible in the background or make it inspectable via `VER MESA FINAL`.
- **RESULT-10** On loss, emphasize what the winner did rather than punitive `you lost` messaging.
- **RESULT-11** Make rematch nearly immediate: retain lineup/settings and transition directly into shuffle/deal.
- **RESULT-12** Keep same-seed/new-seed/debug replay controls out of the mainstream results hierarchy.
- **RESULT-13** Add concise character-specific win/loss reactions.
- **RESULT-14** Do not add XP/currency/daily-reward systems to compensate for weak replay motivation.

Verification: local win/loss/stalemate, stats with/without space, quick rematch, setup-change path, mobile results layout.

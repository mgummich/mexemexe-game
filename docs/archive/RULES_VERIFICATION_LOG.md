## What was not captured

**Draw-pile-empty end screen (single-player e2e)** was skipped: the 108-card deck now leaves a
much larger draw pile than the old 52-card build, and there is no accelerated/mocked draw path
in the single-player showcase (unlike the multiplayer spec, which already drives ~94 draw
rounds over a real WS connection in ~20s). Driving it deterministically in `e2e/screenshot.spec.ts`
would mean ~90+ scripted `comprar()` round trips against the full Phaser scene per test, which is
disproportionate for one screenshot. The equivalent end-of-game path (draw pile exhaustion →
fewest-cards winner) **is** proven end-to-end by `verify:multiplayer`'s `mp-win-a.png` /
`mp-win-b.png` shots and its `revisionsObserved` log (97 revisions from a 94-card draw pile
draining to 0).
